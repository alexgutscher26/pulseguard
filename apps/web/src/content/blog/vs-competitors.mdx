Choosing a monitoring platform is a long-term infrastructure decision. We built SteadyStack after experiencing the frustrations of legacy tools firsthand: slow 5-minute free checks, expensive per-seat pricing models, and noisy 3 AM false alarms.

Here is an objective comparison of how SteadyStack compares to the most popular monitoring services.

## The Three Core SteadyStack Differentiators

### 1. 60-Second Checks as the Baseline

While legacy competitors restrict free accounts to 5-minute sampling (300 seconds), SteadyStack checks all endpoints every 60 seconds. That means up to **5x faster incident detection** and zero blind spots for transient micro-outages.

### 2. Edge Quorum Consensus Voting

Instead of relying on a single probe node, SteadyStack verifies failures across geographically pinned Cloudflare edge Durable Objects in parallel. An alert is only dispatched when multi-region quorum confirms the failure, eliminating false alarm fatigue.

### 3. Flat, Developer-Friendly Pricing

SteadyStack does not charge per seat or penalize growing engineering teams. Paid plans scale on flat monitor capacity with generous limits.
